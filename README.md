🚀 Executive Summary & Key KPIs
Total Customer Base: 6,687 customers analyzed across 50 states.

Active Customers: 4,891 (~73.1%).

Churn Rate: 26.86% (1,796 churned customers).

Average Customer Tenure: 32.3 months.

Total Revenue Lost from Churn: $1.37 Million (Total Lifetime Charges of churned users).

Average Customer Lifetime Value (CLV): $1,084 per customer.

Monthly Revenue at Risk: $66,000 in recurring monthly charges from churned accounts.

🔑 Key Insights & Findings
1. Primary Drivers of Churn (Root Causes)
Competitor Pressure: Accounts for 45.5% of all customer churn ($623K lost revenue), making it the primary threat.

Customer Service & Dissatisfaction:

Customers reaching 4 or 5 customer service calls experience an 88% to 100% churn rate.

Product dissatisfaction and poor service attitude jointly account for 32% of overall churn.

Contract Type Vulnerability:

Month-to-Month contracts generated $909K (66.5%) of total lost revenue.

Long-term contracts (1-year and 2-year) significantly reduce churn risk.

2. Demographic & Pricing Risk Profiles
Senior Citizens (65+): Highest churn rate across age groups at 38% (483 churned customers).

Price Sensitivity: Customers with higher monthly bills ($30 – $60+) show significantly elevated churn rates (up to 37%).

💡 Strategic Recommendations
Proactive Support Interventions: Set up automated triggers in CRM when a customer hits 3 customer service calls to offer VIP assistance before they reach the critical 88% churn threshold.

Month-to-Month Conversion Campaigns: Incentivize users on month-to-month plans to switch to 1- or 2-year contracts with small promotional discounts to improve retention.

Targeted Senior Engagement: Offer tailored digital onboarding and dedicated support for senior customers (65+) who demonstrate the highest churn probability.

Competitive Price Adjustments: Audit service plans in the $40–$60 monthly range to counter competitor poaching strategies.
